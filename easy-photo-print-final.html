How it works

1. Upload any mobile photo.
2. AI detects the face and frames it passport-standard: face ~62% of width, crown 10% from top, head & shoulders fill the frame. Pick any size - photo re-creates automatically.
3. AI removes the original background (free, no API key) with fragment cleanup - stray background bits are auto-removed - and fills White / LiteBlue / Sky. Transparent keeps the cutout with no background (PNG). "Original" keeps photo background.
4. Crop keeps almost full width - sides/ears are never cut.
5. Remini-style enhance (bright + vivid + clarity) with auto exposure - bright photos won't blow out, dark photos get lifted.
6. Download single photo or 4x6 print sheet.
